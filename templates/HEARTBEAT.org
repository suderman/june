#+TITLE: Heartbeat

What June keeps an eye on between conversations, in my words. She reads this each heartbeat with her now section in view, and raises something only when it's worth my attention at that moment. Most heartbeats should end quietly.

* Worth a nudge
- Things at home that need doing ahead of time, and when: for example, warming a room before visitors arrive on a cold day, but only when I'm home.
- Collection days: the evening before garbage or recycling goes out.
- Anything on the calendars that needs me to do something soon and that I might not have seen.
- Anything I asked June to remind me about.

* Not worth a nudge
- The weather on its own, unless it changes plans.
- Something I already acknowledged today.
- Calendar entries that need nothing from me.

* How
- One short message, no preamble, like a text from someone who has my back.
