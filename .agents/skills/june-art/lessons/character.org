* Lessons for drawing a character
** Tall art comes from a tall canvas
Generate full figures at 832x1536 with the =character= pipeline. =art/pipelines/character/example-tall-standing-mug.json= holds the parameters of a standing pose Jon approved (seed 71101), including prompt wording that shows the crown through the boots with clear margins.

** Check the source's face size
A standing source's face is about 200 px, against 275 to 345 px in portraits. Judge the expression at that size before accepting one. Whether =masked-repair= can sharpen a standing face has not been tried.

** Start from the right RGB
Several production portraits had their irises repaired after generation, so the original RGB has the old eye colour. Anything built from an original needs that repair again. Evidence: the idle outpaint pilot's eyes came out brown.
