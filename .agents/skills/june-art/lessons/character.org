* Lessons for drawing a character
** Tall art comes from a tall canvas
Generate full figures at 832x1536 with the =character= pipeline. =art/pipelines/character/example-tall-standing-mug.json= holds the parameters of a standing pose Jon approved (seed 71101). Evidence: all 12 generations of the 2026-11-08 tall-first run were single adult figures with believable proportions, with no sign of the anatomy faults that portrait extension produced.

** Check the top and bottom edges before judging a candidate
Asking for "generous empty space above her hair" did not keep the crown in frame. Of six candidates cut out on 2026-11-08, three touched the top edge (92002 by 110 px, 92003 by 73, 93003 by 30), and 92002's boot touched the bottom. Measure the alpha's top and bottom rows with =magick -alpha extract= before choosing a seed, then inspect the original RGB too. Curious seed 96103 on 2026-11-08 had a visibly clipped crown but zero solid top-edge pixels after ToonOut, with a two-pixel gap. Seed 96102 had 25 solid bottom-edge pixels while =edges= still reported =clear: true=. Read the counts, not just that boolean. A clipped crown needs repair before ingest: full framing stands her on the floor, and =frame= pins a cut crown to the top, so the two conflict.

** Prompt olive-hazel eyes and let the colour step finish them
Jon tried emerald, then rejected bright versions as radioactive on 2026-11-08 and chose the muted olive-green of =gentle-offering-mug-front=. The "olive-hazel green-dominant" wording gave green eyes straight from generation in the 2026-11-08 tall run, without the repairs earlier portraits needed. Do not re-roll for iris colour: =colour-run.org= sets it.

** Size every face box by one rule
Hand-measured boxes on 2026-11-08 ran 1.57 to 1.88 times the eye-to-chin distance, and the two tightest drew her head noticeably larger. Use the rule in =references/tall-pose.org=.

** A small standing face can still read
Forehead-to-chin faces of about 150 px in an 832x1536 source were sharp and held their expression at full panel size after ingest. Judge the expression at that size anyway. Evidence: idle-standing-attentive, seed 91002.

** Do not reject a candidate for its floor shadow
Most candidates drew one despite the prompt and negative. ToonOut removed it in all six cut-outs on 2026-11-08, so judge the cut-out. It does not always remove it: nails 115503 on 2026-11-09 kept grey wisps beside the front boot that only a native boot crop showed. The fix was to clear only the alpha of grey, low-saturation pixels (saturation under 0.3, value over 0.35) in a small box outside the boot. The boot outline and RGB were untouched. Inspect every cut-out's boots at native size.

** The skirt colour wanders
"Dark charcoal-plum" came out olive, charcoal or brown across seeds, and canon is plum-brown. Treat this as a job for colour normalization, not a reason to re-roll.

** Look for changes to her body
Jon's one note on the first tall set (2026-11-08): determined seed 93003 flattened her chest. Compare her figure with canon, not only her face and anatomy.

** A leaning pose suits one side
Curious 96103 leans with her body on the viewer's right of her face, so she reads whole standing on the panel's right, and her body falls behind the words on the left. Report which side a sideways pose suits.

** Bodies vary in face heights
Standing figures measure up to ten face heights tall, against about seven and a half for the older clipboards, so framing must fit the figure rather than assume a share of the panel. Measure each new pose.

** Start from the right RGB
Several production portraits had their irises repaired after generation, so the original RGB has the old eye colour. Anything built from an original needs that repair again. Evidence: the idle outpaint pilot's eyes came out brown.

** Name the direction actually drawn
Check her face, not just her body's lean. All four curious-standing-lean seeds on 2026-11-08 looked toward the viewer despite left-facing and three-quarter wording. Seed 96103 was staged as =curious-standing-lean-front= with =facing: front= and =mirror: false=; the left-facing inventory slot remains unfilled. In the gentle-standing-hands-clasped run later that day, only 97103 of four seeds gave a shallow viewer-left face turn. It also had a complete native crown with 22 px of top clearance and intact boots, so no crown repair was needed. Keep the valid directional take rather than spending repair allowance on an already complete head; the other three were frontal.

** Correct the failed limb instruction without claiming the direction was fixed
All four first pout seeds on 2026-11-09 put her hands behind her body. One four-seed retry explicitly placed both forearms across the blouse and each hand on the opposite upper arm, with hidden hands in the negative. All four drew visibly folded arms, but none achieved the requested looking-away face. Seed 103803 became =pout-standing-folded-front=, not the directional inventory entry. A successful hand correction is not evidence of successful yaw.

** Use the reviewed side pair for a left-facing experiment
The existing =june-directional-side-body.png= and =june-directional-side-face.png= inputs, paired with an upright head and nose pointing viewer-left, produced genuinely turned idle, wry and pout candidates in tall batch 2 on 2026-11-09. Selected seeds 112102, 112201 and 112302 retained June's outfit and headset without mirroring or new crops. This supports left yaw with that pair, not right yaw or a new reference family. Resolve its historical hashes through the later approved body/face promotion records before use.

** Say where a facepalm touches
The first four facepalm seeds in tall batch 2 rested the hand at her cheek or ear. One four-seed retry specified palm across the brow, fingers reaching the hairline and no cheek/ear support. Seed 113602 clearly performs the forehead gesture. The useful correction was contact location, not more seeds of the same prompt.

** Closed eyes can break a bashful direct gaze, but change the feeling
All four initial downcast-clasp seeds in tall batch 2 still looked at the viewer. One retry asked for a lowered head and closed eyes. Seed 113802 stopped the flirtatious eye contact, but its pinched mouth reads anxious rather than softly smiling. Report that tradeoff; successful gaze correction does not certify the intended emotion.

** Specify a modest opening without adding another garment
Tall batch 2 replaced the blouse's open-collar phrase with a modestly open neckline, collar open at the throat and chest covered by the blouse. The eleven selected poses kept the rose blouse without the deep opening of the first tall batch or a dark undershirt. Keep the identity and outfit wording otherwise unchanged.

** Name her figure in every prompt, not only the one Jon flagged
Jon rejected the 2026-11-09 shrug for its cleavage and large chest. Adding "slender adult figure with a modest natural bust like the reference" plus =large breasts, busty= in the negative fixed all four redraw seeds without flattening her. In the same run, the surprised prompt lacked the figure line, and three of four seeds ballooned. The earcup's forward lean exaggerated the bust until a retry added the line. Put it in every prompt.

** Keep the boom away from a wide-open mouth
Three of four first unhinged seeds on 2026-11-09 drew the headset boom as a black gag over her open mouth. The retry placed the boom "angled down beside her cheek well away from her open mouth", with =gag, mouth covered, microphone covering mouth= in the negative. None of the four retry seeds repeated the fault.

** Text does not make lopsided gag eyes
Eight seeds asked for one huge eye and one small crooked eye with mismatched pupils. Every one drew symmetric eyes, and the result read as manic glee. Promising that face needs a different method, such as an edit pass or a sketch reference. More seeds will not produce it.

** Spell out worried brows for a cringe
All four first cringe seeds paired the clenched-teeth grimace with lowered, angry brows and fists. The retry asked for worried brows "raised high and slanted upward in the middle" and limp, curled hands, and put =angry, furrowed brows, fists= in the negative. Three of four then read as cringing, not angry.

** Baked reaction marks still appear
Two of four hair-twirl seeds drew a thought puff or squiggle beside her head, despite =decorative marks= in the negative. Reject those seeds, and put reaction marks in the manifest's =marks=, which the renderer draws.

** A bored look stays on the object
For the nail inspection, Jon preferred 115501, whose eyes never leave her hand, over 115503's half-lidded sideways glance, which the agent had picked. For bored or absorbed performances, a gaze fixed on the prop reads stronger than a knowing look aside.
