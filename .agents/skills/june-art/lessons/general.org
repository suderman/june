* Lessons for every art job
Rules learned from real runs, each with its evidence. Read this and the lessons for your pipeline before starting. Before setting EVAL, add what this job taught you, including what failed, and remove a rule that a later result disproved. Keep each to a rule and its evidence; the Org thread holds the story.

** If the same fault appears across seeds, change the method
Seeds vary a composition; they do not repair a method. Evidence: all four first-step seeds of the idle outpaint pilot (2026-11-08) failed at the join.

** A technical pass is not an art pass
Pixel checks prove protected art survived, not that the new art is good. Look at the actual image at full size against the canon. Evidence: the bashful crown repair completed cleanly and invented a floating cap. Curious crown 96202 also passed edge and protected-RGB checks but drew a detached second crown; 96201 was the coherent repair.

** Check the graph that ran, not the one you meant
A positional connection silently left =ImageCompositeMasked= without its mask, and five outputs used the whole decoded image. =june-art run= now refuses this graph.

** Give the model a whole subject to continue
Evidence, not yet isolated from other causes: idle outpaint windows that showed only 448 px of belt and skirt produced three legs (81002) and two lower bodies (81003).

** Gate on the first subject before spending the rest
Run one subject's seeds and look at them before the others, then carry what they teach into the next prompts. Evidence: the tall-first run's idle gate (2026-11-08) proved the method and exposed the headroom problem after 4 of 12 generations.

** Render the real panel before accepting the art
A clean image can still lay out wrongly. Evidence: the first full-figure pose ran off the bottom of the tallest panels until =frame= learned to fit her, which only the private =Panel.qml= proof showed. The curious-standing-lean-front proof on 2026-11-08 exposed a different fault: right-side long panels at 1040, 820 and 620 px selected =full= but cropped the tucked hand, and the latter two cut much of her body at the right edge. Her source and normalized silhouette were intact. =full= is not a certificate that every limb fits; record the renderer fault rather than changing measured face geometry.

** Resolve stale reference hashes through approval records
A changed hash is a stop for investigation, not permission to substitute an image. In the 2026-11-08 curious run, the sheet hash in =references/environment.org= was stale after the approved emerald-eye promotion. The canonical manifest and promotion record explained the change, and the face/body files matched both the current sheet's exact crops and server inputs. Without that approval chain, do not generate.

** Check which geometry a proof helper models
=examples/preflight-pose.mjs= calls =placePose('pose')=, even for full-figure sources. In the curious run it predicted 2816x5386, while the actual tall ingest used 2048x4096. Use the real tall placement and resulting manifest for tall-art evidence; do not treat that portrait-only report as a failed or passed tall normalization gate.
